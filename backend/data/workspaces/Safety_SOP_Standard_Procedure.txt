# SAFETY SOP-17: INDUSTRIAL PRESSURE RELIEF VALVES & PUMP INSPECTION

1. PURPOSE & SCOPE
This Standard Operating Procedure (SOP) outlines safety protocols for pressure relief valve (PRV) testing and turbine pump maintenance across high-pressure refining units.

2. MANDATORY TOLERANCES & METRICS
- Maximum Operating Pressure Ceiling: 120.0 PSI
- Allowable Pressure Variance: +/- 5%
- Inspection Interval: Mandatory calibration every 90 days.
- Over-pressure Threshold: Any reading exceeding 135.0 PSI constitutes a CRITICAL SAFETY DEVIATION requiring immediate shutdown and isolation.

3. INSPECTION & APPROVAL PROCEDURES
- Step 1: Perform visual check for valve seat erosion or seal leakage.
- Step 2: Record pressure transducer gauge metrics.
- Step 3: Run python diagnostic verification formula: P_delta = (P_measured - P_baseline) / P_baseline.
- Step 4: If P_delta > 0.12, file an official Engineering Approval Note requiring Human-in-the-Loop operator sign-off before restart.
